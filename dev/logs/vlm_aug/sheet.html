Two limits this sheet does not get to ignore

  1. A photograph supports qualitative comparison and no measurement at all. Unknown camera position, unknown lens, converging verticals, a roof seen from below. Every number printed beside a render below came out of the IR. No number anywhere on this page was read off a photograph, and none should be.
  2. A visual check is a tripwire, not a passing grade. "That looks wrong" is strong evidence something is broken. "That looks right" is weak evidence of nothing much. Systematic small error is invisible here by construction and belongs to the dimensional assertions.

The photographs' situations are also partly estimated — the corpus records an address, not a street width. Each pair prints the provenance of the width it was generated from. A height disagreement on an estimated row is a question about this harness's table before it is a question about the generator.
