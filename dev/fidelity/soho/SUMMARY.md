# SoHo cast iron: three review rounds

R is recognisability and P is plausibility, each out of 5. Each round's key sits beside its images.

| Round | Method | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | A photo-only observer, an adversary testing the rules against photographs, and a grader | Ironwork D. Proportions, openings, ground floor, top, ornament and variety C. Colour B (`grading.md`) |
| Round 2 | 16 blind drawings, two judges | Fair judge: R 3.6, P 2.6 (iron fronts P 3, masonry P 2), none clean. Sceptical judge: R 3.4, P 2.8, 5 fail (four brick lofts without lintels, a pediment on plain brick) |
| Round 3 | 16 fresh blind drawings, two new judges | Fair judge: R 3.75, P 3.0, three at P 4. Sceptical judge: none fail, all 16 with flaws |

## After round 3 (applied, not judged again)

- **Stair door:** the door and its transom now fill the opening up to the store's lintel, with no blank panel over the door.
- **Columns:** loft columns have real capitals (0.38 m) and bases.
- **Cornice:** it projects 1.4 m on iron fronts and 1.2 m on brick ones, over a deeper frieze. A brick top floor leaves 0.3 m of wall under it.
- **Storey heights:** upper floors diminish about 4% a floor.
- **Fire escapes:**
  - Each stair now runs at about 60° across one bay, drawn as two stringers.
  - Fire escapes appear on 70% of iron fronts.

## Still open
