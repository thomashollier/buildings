R is recognisability and P is plausibility, each out of 5. In both blind rounds the judges placed the drawings in Havana. In round 3 the fair judge rated img04, img06 and img07 as close to real fronts. Both rejected the two-bay colonial fronts under hipped roofs (img01, img09, img13).

## Round 3: what the judges found, and the final refinements

The rules were refined after round 3. These refinements were not judged again.

1. **A band of dark squares over the ground floor.** This was the bodega sign's lettering. The sign is now a plain board on a quarter of bodegas, and only where the corbels above clear it.
2. **The same panelled door with a flat grey slab in every ground bay.**
   - A zaguán with a flat head now has an iron-grilled transom.
   - A bodega's openings take louvres over panels in the house's leaf colour under a grilled transom, or roll shutters. They no longer copy the zaguán's door.
3. **Too much blank wall over the upper openings (34–43% of the storey).** Upper openings are now 0.70–0.78 of the storey, still clearing the balcony corbels above.
4. **Too few bays on wide fronts.** Bays are now 3.0–4.4 m (colonial) and 3.0–4.0 m (later). A 12 m lot carries 3 openings.
5. **Guardavecinos drawn as a flat fan.** They are now drawn edge on, as a narrow iron bar with spikes at the balcony's ends.
6. **Pretil dies off the pilaster axes.** The pretil's dies now stand on the pilaster axes and at the corners, with urns on them where the pretil has urns.
7. **Hipped tile roofs between party walls.** Hipped roofs are now limited to 30% of two-storey colonial houses without an entresuelo.
8. **The colonial fan in a rectangular head.** It is now a straight iron-grilled transom. Fans stay under round heads.
9. **Glazed entresuelo windows.** These are now square windows with louvred leaves.
10. **Floor hierarchy.**
    - The principal floor is 8–15% taller than the floor above it.
    - On a tall front the top floor is about 10% lower than a standard floor and has no hoods.
11. **Faceted crests.** The cartouche, the segmental crest and the oval are now drawn as smooth curves.
12. **The arcade read as blind.** The wall behind a portal is now in deep shadow.
