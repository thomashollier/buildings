Reference Selection Review

Two limits, kept in view while selecting: photographs support qualitative comparison reliably — proportion, ornament family, silhouette, rhythm — and support measurement not at all. And a visual check is a tripwire, not a passing grade: "that looks wrong" is strong evidence something is broken; "that looks right" is weak evidence anything is actually correct. Select images that give the clearest, least ambiguous read on the feature they are meant to evidence — not necessarily the prettiest photo.
